October twenty fourth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Ancient Brain Virus, First Lady's Ceasefire, and Sauna Science.

First, a viral pandemic that spread roughly five to seven million years ago may explain differences between human and chimpanzee brains, according to a study published this week. Chimpanzees, our closest relatives, share nearly ninety nine percent of our genes, yet the human brain is roughly three times larger, a gap that has puzzled scientists for decades. The study found a fragment of viral DNA in chimpanzees, but not humans, that appears to impair genes vital to brain development. Researchers believe an ancient retrovirus similar to HIV inserted its genetic material into primate genomes, while humans may have been protected by an antiviral protein that blocked it. Separately, another study found chimpanzees are less likely to steal food from friends than from enemies, which may shed light on the roots of fairness and friendship.

The Justice Department sued to block Alaska's Senate results if incumbent Republican Dan Sullivan loses, arguing another candidate with the same name could mislead voters. Alaska's Supreme Court previously rejected an attempt to remove the other Dan Sullivan from the ballot, and recent polls show former Democratic Representative Mary Peltola narrowly ahead.

Separately, the Justice Department charged two former FBI officials with lying to Congress about their roles in the agency's Hunter Biden investigations.

New York City banned horse drawn carriages in Central Park, with the city council voting to eliminate them by June twenty twenty eight. The fight intensified this summer after an eighteen year old tourist died in a carriage accident, and the ban leaves questions about roughly two hundred workers and one hundred eighty horses.

Melania Trump helped broker a localized Russia Ukraine ceasefire, the White House says, allowing civilian evacuations and aid deliveries to Oleshky next week. The Ukrainian city lacks electricity, heat, food and running water, and about two thousand residents remain, down from twenty four thousand before the war.

Separately, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says Starlink is still not fully working, despite reports that President Donald Trump asked Elon Musk to lift restrictions.

Israel ordered a full lockdown of the occupied West Bank after a shootout killed two Israeli soldiers and two suspected Palestinian gunmen. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said soldiers will search homes in the Hebron neighborhood where the suspects lived, ahead of Israel's general election.

French officials deployed seventy thousand law enforcement officers ahead of planned protests over rising living costs, closing the Louvre and other major museums. At least three thousand storefronts were looted or vandalized in last week's protests, and the State Department warned Americans traveling to Paris for Sunday's Steelers and Saints NFL game.
