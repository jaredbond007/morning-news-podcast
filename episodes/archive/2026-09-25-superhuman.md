Google plans to launch its first AI satellite on October first under Project Suncatcher, its effort to scale AI computing with data centers in orbit that capture solar energy nearly around the clock. The test satellite is about the size of a refrigerator and carries four of Google's Tensor Processing Units, the chips it uses for AI, to see how they perform in space. The team still has to solve engineering hurdles like keeping the hardware alive, cooling it in a vacuum and linking satellites together.
