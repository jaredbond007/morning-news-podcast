November nineteenth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Trump, Xi, Putin Summit, Grand Theft Auto, and Animal Emissions.

Breaking news first: President Trump was scheduled to begin a three way meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin this morning, at one thirty Eastern time.

Anthropic makes its Nasdaq debut today under the ticker ANT, after pricing its shares yesterday at eighty three dollars, above its expected range of seventy to seventy eight. The AI company raised sixty eight and a half billion dollars at a one point five trillion dollar valuation, the second largest IPO ever after SpaceX, which raised about eighty six billion at a one point eight trillion valuation. Founded in twenty twenty one by former OpenAI employees, Anthropic set itself apart as a safety first AI company, and CEO Dario Amodei has pushed for guardrails against cyberattacks, weapons development and rogue AI agents while touting AI's potential to cure diseases. Claude grew more popular this year after the Pentagon blacklisted it over its military restrictions, briefly pushing it past ChatGPT in downloads. The decision to go public was driven largely by soaring computing costs, with nearly nine hundred billion dollars in spending commitments through twenty forty five.

Grand Theft Auto six hit consoles at midnight local time today, starting a rolling global launch thirteen years in the making. Rockstar Games' latest has already brought in more than one point six billion dollars from seventeen million preorders. The open world sequel has six hundred thousand animations and the series' first required female lead, Lucia, voiced by Manni L. Perez. Time off requests for launch day jumped nearly thirteen percent compared with the same dates the past two years, with about one in five salaried workers planning to take the day off to play. Analysts estimate the game could earn three to five billion dollars in its first week.

Shoppers have been panic buying pantry staples and generators at Costco, investment firm Mizuho Securities reported this week, raising its price target on the stock from one thousand one hundred to one thousand one hundred fifty dollars. Mizuho says the trend began in late October and is largely tied to social media rumors of a blackout that has not happened. Recent outages at Microsoft, Apple, Chase, United and the FAA have added to fears that US infrastructure is vulnerable to cyberattacks, though Secretary of State Marco Rubio and other officials have said the outages are not connected. Costco has seen jumps in rice, canned goods, water, paper products, batteries and generators, and Mizuho expects Walmart, Sam's Club, Home Depot and Lowe's may also benefit.

In science and technology. Researchers built insect sized flying robots with onboard sensors and computers to navigate on their own, with possible uses in pollinating plants and search and rescue. Fossil teeth suggest Homo erectus may have lived in rainforests far longer than thought and coexisted with other humans. Separately, giant eagles were found preying on baboons of nearly sixty pounds, raising the possibility they hunted similarly sized early humans. And the National Institutes of Health will move the last federally owned chimpanzees in US research facilities to a sanctuary next year, having stopped supporting invasive chimp research in twenty fifteen.

In business and markets. US stocks closed mixed, with the S and P five hundred down two tenths of a percent, the Dow down one point one and the Nasdaq up four tenths, as Federal Reserve minutes from October showed policymakers expect a quarter point rate hike in December. Pending home sales fell four point seven percent in October to their lowest since two thousand one, a second straight month of record lows. And TJX, owner of TJ Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods, saw shares jump eight percent on better than expected third quarter revenue of sixteen billion dollars and four percent comparable store sales growth.

In politics and world affairs. House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries for speaker in the next Congress, and he could become the first Black speaker, pending a full House vote. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin answered criticism for flying a government plane to a college football game after Friday's ground stop, saying he paid for part of the flight and is required to fly government aircraft. And Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth says the US and Venezuela agreed to restore military ties, including intelligence sharing.
