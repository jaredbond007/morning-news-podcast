October fourth, twenty twenty six. The Superhuman newsletter. SpaceX just cleared a massive hurdle.

This is Superhuman's Sunday Special, covering science and tech news outside of AI. First, SpaceX's Starship reached orbit. On its fourteenth test flight, the giant rocket entered orbit for the first time and deployed twenty six Starlink V three satellites along the way. The mission was cut short, landing after about ninety minutes instead of the planned ten hours. It is still a big win, because NASA is counting on Starship to land astronauts on the moon for Artemis, and a major failure would have squeezed an already tight timeline.

Next, scientists identified the first new penguin species in more than a century. Researchers studying gentoo penguins found they are actually four distinct species, one of them previously unknown. But they project three of the four, including the new species, will lose significant habitat to climate change, while only the southern gentoo in Antarctica may expand its range.

Then, your walls could soon make electricity from humid air. Scientists say they built a paper based wallpaper packed with tiny moisture electric generators that pull water vapor from indoor air and turn it into a steady voltage. A full size sheet with one thousand five hundred ninety six units powered a wireless keyboard while also lowering the room's humidity. It will not run big appliances, but it could keep battery free sensors, thermostats and other small smart home devices going.

And a medieval potion is giving scientists new ideas against antibiotic resistance. Researchers recreated Bald's eyesalve, a tenth century remedy made from garlic, onion, cow bile and wine, and found it damages bacterial membranes, blocks communication between bacteria, and weakens the protective films they build. Most striking, bacteria took far longer to develop resistance to it. Scientists are not suggesting anyone brew it at home, but hope to turn its key ingredients into a safer lab made version.

In new tech gadgets this week: Meta's Muse Charm, a Tamagotchi style device for talking to Meta's AI agent without your phone; Sudo, a dedicated music player built for offline listening with physical controls; Kamingo, a compact converter that turns a regular bike into an electric bike in under ten seconds; and Freckle, a phone for kids seven to thirteen with no browser, app store or social media.

Trending this week, an octopus that noticed it was being followed changed color until it nearly vanished into the seafloor, and a rare pigment mutation produced a bright orange shark that is going viral again.

And finally, some good news. Students at Eindhoven University unveiled VENTRA, a concept electric car that electrically charges tire and brake particles near the wheels and pulls them onto a surface for cleaning and recycling, targeting pollution even exhaust free cars release. And scientists say they found a possibly reversible weakness in Alzheimer's biggest risk gene, which damages the brain's blood vessels by turning support cells into scar forming ones. The results are early, but they point to new treatment targets.
