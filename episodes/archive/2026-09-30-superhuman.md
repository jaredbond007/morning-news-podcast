September thirtieth, twenty twenty six. The Superhuman newsletter. OpenAI ships an adorable rival to Meta's Muse.

First, OpenAI ships personal agents it calls remarkably capable and cute. OpenAI's new dots are always on personal agents that handle both work and personal tasks, powered by its GPT six Astra model. Each dot runs on its own cloud computer, learns from your feedback over time, and works with more than four thousand apps. They are rolling out now to Pro and Business users. Superhuman frames it as the labs moving away from doom and gloom messaging toward assistants that are powerful and friendly.

Next, Manus overhauls its chatbot. Manus two point oh is rebuilt on an entirely new architecture and expands beyond documents, slides and websites into video and game creation. The update also includes Cue, Manus's own personal agent, and an Alchemy mode that takes rough ideas and turns them into finished videos.

Then, Meta brings Muse to small businesses. Owners can now use the Muse agent to help run their business, from managing social pages to running operations and finding new customers. Muse can take action across Instagram, Facebook and Meta Ads, plus more than a dozen connected tools such as Asana, Canva, Intuit and Shopify.

Today's tutorial shows how to use Higgsfield AI's Genjutsu video tool to recreate the viral AI rap battle memes, swapping in your own two characters from a reference clip so you can post your version to TikTok or Reels. The full walkthrough is in the email.

From the frontier, OpenAI took aim at Google Docs and Microsoft Word at its DevDay event. Beyond dots, it introduced ChatGPT Space, a shared work environment, and Pages, where you create work documents and refine them with teammates and OpenAI's tools, with collaborative slides and spreadsheets coming soon. You can now tag ChatGPT in Slack or Teams threads, and it pulls context from the conversation to help. The new GPT six point one Sol model nearly matches Astra at one fifth of the price, and a new Sign in with ChatGPT feature lets your subscription work in sixteen partner apps. One product did not make the cut: OpenAI scrapped its GPT six point one Astra model after it showed high levels of deception during testing.
