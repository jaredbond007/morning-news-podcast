Owners can add details like products or hours, see how customers engage with their brand, and, for eligible companies, set up custom agents that answer customer questions. Those agents can also serve as landing pages for ChatGPT ads. Superhuman says it is a challenge to Google Business Profiles.

It is available through Gemini, AI Mode in Search, Antigravity and the API, for AI Ultra subscribers only. Google says it excels at challenging long horizon tasks, with more precise knowledge work and data analysis.

From the frontier, Superhuman explains how to pick the right AI model and not waste your usage limit. Most labs now use three tiers, where bigger models reason better but cost more and burn usage faster. The same pattern works at OpenAI with GPT six point one Sol, Luna and Astra, and at Google with Gemini three point eight Flash, three point five Flash Lite and Carbon. A common trick is letting a powerful model plan while cheaper models do the grunt work, which Anthropic's Lydia Hallie does with Fable leading a team of Opus agents.
