November thirteenth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Nvidia Shakeup, NYC Building Collapse, and Friday the Thirteenth.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned yesterday amid reports of extramarital affairs and an investigation into whether he was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while the company searches for a successor. Huang had admitted to infidelity amid reports linking him to Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore based Nvidia customer Megaspeed whom US officials suspect of spying for China. The New York Times reported a second affair Wednesday with a senior executive who reported directly to him, and Reuters reported allegations of a third yesterday, after he resigned. Independent directors and outside counsel are examining possible espionage risks, the FBI is running a separate inquiry, and Nvidia has lost more than five hundred fifty billion dollars in market value since its board announced an investigation Sunday. Huang cofounded Nvidia in nineteen ninety three and built it into an artificial intelligence powerhouse.

Rescue crews finished searching the rubble of a collapsed Brooklyn apartment building yesterday as the death toll rose to eight. New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani said two children were among the dead and at least eighteen people were injured. The three story building crumbled into a neighboring construction site around four in the afternoon Wednesday, and firefighters arrived within a minute of the nine one one call, with more than two hundred fifty emergency workers freeing trapped survivors overnight. Heavy rain hampered the effort, muddying the site and grounding two police drones. Records show the adjacent construction site was cited in March for violations including unauthorized demolition and inadequate protection of neighboring buildings. The city's Department of Buildings is investigating the cause, and Brooklyn's district attorney is looking into whether criminal conduct played a role.

Johnny Depp returns to the big screen today as Ebenezer Scrooge in Paramount's adaptation of Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol, his first major Hollywood role since his legal battle with ex wife Amber Heard, who accused him of domestic abuse. Ebenezer cost thirty eight million dollars to make and is expected to bring in about forty million worldwide on its opening weekend. Its performance is widely seen as a test of whether Depp can still draw audiences after the twenty twenty libel trial in the UK and the twenty twenty two defamation trial in the US. Depp, who denies the allegations, reportedly took a pay cut to five million dollars, compared with sixteen million for the twenty eighteen Fantastic Beasts, and Disney is reportedly weighing a Pirates of the Caribbean revival with him. Dickens' eighteen forty three novella has inspired more than one hundred adaptations.
