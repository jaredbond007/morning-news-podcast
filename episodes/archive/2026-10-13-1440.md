And finally, a fire destroyed Paris' oldest covered food market early yesterday, and the cause is not yet clear. The Enfants Rouges, or red children, market sits in the city's historic Marais neighborhood and was founded in sixteen fifteen under King Louis the thirteenth, later named for a nearby orphanage whose children wore red uniforms. Residents in the nineteen eighties and nineties blocked a plan to turn it into a parking lot, and it reopened as a food court in two thousand. This year Food and Wine named it one of the world's top ten market destinations, and all seventeen of its stalls were destroyed.
