October sixteenth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Ukraine Attacks Resume, Elizabeth Holmes, and Dog Week.

First, the energy ceasefire is over. Ukraine struck an oil refinery southeast of Moscow yesterday in retaliation for Russia damaging energy facilities in and around Kyiv, defying President Donald Trump's declaration of a ceasefire on Sunday. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says he was not involved in any ceasefire talks before Trump's announcement, but he paused strikes for several days, and alleged yesterday that US officials had threatened to withhold intelligence if he kept attacking. Russia never publicly acknowledged a ceasefire, and an attack Wednesday left more than six hundred thousand Ukrainians without power. Ukraine says it got no advance warning of that attack and accused Elon Musk's SpaceX of cutting its access to the Starshield internet service. Brent crude futures fell a tenth of a percent yesterday, while West Texas Intermediate climbed two point eight percent.

Next, President Trump called off the Pentagon's plan to livestream the firing squad execution of Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan after bipartisan backlash. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth had announced last week that the December third execution would be broadcast to deter terrorism. Hasan, a former Army psychiatrist, killed thirteen people and wounded thirty two at the Texas military base in twenty oh nine. He would have been the first person publicly executed in the US since nineteen thirty six, and about eight in ten Americans oppose public executions, according to recent polling. His execution will still be the first by a US military firing squad since nineteen forty five, as the US also reintroduces electric chair and gas chamber executions.

And finally, a documentary about disgraced Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, You Can See Everything, opens in select theaters today after setting advance ticket sales records for documentaries and A twenty four films at Alamo Drafthouse. The one hundred seventy four minute film begins thirty four days before Holmes entered prison in twenty twenty three to serve an eleven year sentence for defrauding investors. Holmes approached A twenty four about documenting her final weeks of freedom, and the studio tapped comedian Nathan Fielder and filmmaker Lance Oppenheim, who moved into the guesthouse at the San Diego rental she shared with partner Billy Evans and their two children. The filmmakers challenge her account of the scandal while she maintains her innocence. It has a perfect critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes so far and is projected to earn at least seven million dollars on its opening weekend.

And archaeologists unearthed the remains of a Neanderthal likely killed with a wooden spear about seventy five thousand years ago, the oldest known evidence of such lethal violence among human ancestors.
