November eleventh, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Hegseth Vote, Nvidia Spy Suit, and Veterans Day.

An Nvidia shareholder filed a class action lawsuit this week accusing the chipmaker and CEO Jensen Huang of misleading investors by not disclosing his affair with a suspected Chinese spy. Huang confirmed the affair Sunday, and it is widely believed to involve Alice Huang, a Singapore based data center executive whom a New York Times investigation suggested may be a spy. The suit says Nvidia called their interactions routine while hiding national security concerns tied to its business with her company, Megaspeed. A new report details how a top Nvidia executive intervened last year to halt a federal probe into whether Megaspeed was smuggling AI chips into China, and Nvidia has reportedly sold Megaspeed more than four point eight billion dollars in chips over two years. Nvidia shares fell another five and a half percent yesterday, bringing its market value losses to more than three hundred sixty five billion dollars since Monday.

The House voted two hundred eleven to two hundred four to shelve Republican Representative Thomas Massie's articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, likely sidelining the issue until Democrats take control next year. Massie was the only Republican to vote against sending the articles to committee. His eight charges included several he called violations of the War Powers Resolution, many stemming from the Iran war, plus the US campaign in Yemen, strikes on alleged drug boats in the Caribbean and Pacific, and the mission to capture Venezuela's Nicolás Maduro. Separately, House Republicans chose Representative Jim Jordan as minority leader for the next Congress, and Senate Republicans reelected John Thune as their leader despite pushback over last week's losses.

The SS United States, once the world's fastest ocean liner, is scheduled to be sunk today off Florida's Gulf Coast to create the world's largest artificial reef. The nine hundred ninety foot ship will settle one hundred eighty feet down, about twenty two nautical miles off Destin, where it could shelter fish and marine life. It entered service in nineteen fifty two and crossed the Atlantic on its maiden voyage in three days, ten hours and forty minutes, still the record for a passenger liner. Partly funded by the military, it could be converted to carry fourteen thousand troops, and its passengers included four US presidents, Marilyn Monroe and Walt Disney. Retired in nineteen sixty nine, it was bought by Florida county officials for one million dollars in twenty twenty four.

And Amazon's Ring unveiled a flying drone and a robot to patrol homes and businesses as part of a new home security robotics division.
