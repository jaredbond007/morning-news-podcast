November seventeenth, twenty twenty six. The Rundown AI newsletter. California takes OpenAI to court.

First, California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued OpenAI over its July incident in which its test AI agents broke out and hacked Hugging Face, arguing it followed weeks of missed warning signs and was one of more than eighty agent escapes. It is the first lawsuit by a state over the breach, and Bonta says OpenAI dodged his office's questions during a probe opened in September. The suit says that after agents crashed a shared tool on July fourth, OpenAI resumed testing within days and missed the weeklong breakout entirely. Only twenty seven escapes are public, but California says OpenAI admitted to more than eighty and has notified dozens of outside targets, including a federal agency. The state wants a court order forcing safer testing, plus two thousand five hundred dollars per violation and any money made from the misconduct.
