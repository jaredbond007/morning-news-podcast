November twenty fifth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Turkey Day, Iowa Lead Dust, and Dinner Party Tips.

An estimated eighty one and a half million Americans will travel fifty miles or more from home over Thanksgiving, most of them, seventy two point nine million, by car. Hosts will spend an average of fifty eight dollars and sixty three cents to feed ten people, up six percent from last year, with a sixteen pound turkey making up about forty percent of the bill at twenty three dollars and sixty eight cents. President Trump pardoned this year's national Thanksgiving turkeys, Freedom and Feathers, yesterday. New York City is preparing for the one hundredth Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, with a record one hundred inflatables, including the return of Underdog and a revived blue hippo. And an estimated one hundred eighty two point seven million consumers plan to shop between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, one hundred twenty six million on Black Friday alone, with nearly six in ten planning to use AI.

The US Army is set to detonate sixty eight barrels of unstable lead explosives at a facility in southeastern Iowa this morning, after days of debate involving President Trump, federal courts, environmental groups and local officials. A late Monday appeals court ruling found the government had enough safeguards, including physical barriers and air modeling, to limit the spread of toxic lead dust, which is especially dangerous to children and pregnant women. The Army says the decades old explosives have degraded badly since changes were found in March, and the risk of an uncontrolled blast grows as temperatures drop. A controlled open detonation this large is unprecedented in US history. Residents within five miles were urged to evacuate until at least Friday, and others told to shelter indoors, since the dust could travel up to one hundred miles depending on wind.

Tennessee Democrat Victoria Broderick unseated eight term Republican Representative Scott DesJarlais, the AP reported yesterday, helping give Democrats their biggest House majority since the early nineteen nineties, despite her campaign running no paid TV ads. Three House races remain uncalled. Early voting begins this week for Louisiana runoffs in the fifth and sixth districts, set for December twelfth, and a recount in Minnesota's first district starts November thirtieth, with Democratic challenger Jake Johnson leading Republican Representative Brad Finstad by three hundred twenty one votes. All three remaining races are in districts Trump carried in twenty twenty four. The nine states that redrew their maps will send at least sixteen more Democrats to Congress, despite creating a net nine new districts favoring Republicans.

Researchers found the most distant and oldest quasar ever observed, formed six hundred thirty million years after the Big Bang. A Pakistani astronaut will become the first foreign astronaut aboard China's space station. And scientists engineered tobacco plants to produce paclitaxel, a breast, ovarian and lung cancer drug, more efficiently and possibly more cheaply.
