November twenty third, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Delta Delays, Math Breakthrough, and a Mini Moon.

Hundreds of Delta flights were delayed yesterday after a technical issue prompted an FAA ground stop and later a ground delay for Delta bound flights at Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport. The problem, resolved by evening, was in Delta's weight and balance system, which calculates a plane's total weight, center of gravity and weight distribution. The cause is still unclear, and Delta temporarily used a manual workaround to keep passengers safe. Nearly one thousand Delta flights leave Hartsfield Jackson on peak travel days, so the hourslong outage rippled across the country. The world's busiest airport handled one hundred six point three million fliers in twenty twenty five and expects more than four million this Thanksgiving season, up five percent from last year.

A mathematician employed by Anthropic, Levent Alpöge, says he solved the one hundred sixty seven year old Riemann Hypothesis by working with the company's AI, the second time this year AI has helped crack a Millennium Prize Problem. Independent researchers confirmed the proof over the weekend. In eighteen fifty nine, German mathematician Bernhard Riemann proposed a pattern explaining how prime numbers are distributed and spaced as numbers grow toward infinity, along with a formula to estimate how many primes exist below any number. Proving it was so hard because the pattern had to hold across infinitely many primes without exception. Alpöge guided Claude to express a proof spanning more than seventeen million lines of code, about two months after OpenAI said its system produced a proof for the Navier Stokes problem.

Bayer is on track to pay up to seven point three billion dollars to resolve about sixty five thousand claims that its Roundup weedkiller causes a type of blood cancer. The deal, approved by a Missouri judge, follows a twenty twenty settlement of about ten billion dollars across more than one hundred thousand claims. Eligible claimants exposed through farming, landscaping or home lawn care get tiered payouts averaging ten thousand to one hundred sixty five thousand dollars, covering existing and future non Hodgkin lymphoma diagnoses. At issue is glyphosate, which Bayer began removing from residential Roundup in twenty twenty three; the EPA says it is unlikely to cause cancer when used as directed, though a twenty nineteen analysis of the most highly exposed groups found it may raise the risk by up to forty one percent. Experts say occasional lawn use often poses minimal danger, since risk is tied mainly to prolonged exposure.
