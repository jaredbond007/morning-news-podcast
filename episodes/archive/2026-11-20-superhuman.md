November twentieth, twenty twenty six. The Superhuman newsletter. Alljoined's new model reads brainwaves.

First, Anthropic became the first stand alone US AI lab to go public. Trading under the ticker ANT, its shares jumped roughly seventeen percent on the first day, even as all three major stock indexes fell. Sources told CNBC that demand from retail investors was the largest for any IPO in history. Anthropic also released a video marking the milestone that drew more than six million views.

Next, Alljoined, a startup aiming to decode human thoughts, revealed AIR O, a model that reads noninvasive brain signals and generates an image of the object a person is focusing on, in real time. When the person's focus shifts, the picture changes. The demo has not been independently verified, but it hints at a future where computers could respond directly to human thoughts.

Then, OpenAI is rolling out ChatGPT Health across all plan tiers and most platforms, including the desktop app, dots, Sites and Android for US users. The broader rollout lets you connect your health information across apps. For example, you could analyze sleep patterns from a wearable, track them on a dashboard built in Sites, and have your dot send proactive tips based on what it finds.
