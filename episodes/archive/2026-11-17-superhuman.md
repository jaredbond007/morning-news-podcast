November seventeenth, twenty twenty six. The Superhuman newsletter. Maps get powerful new features.

First, Google made Ask Maps, its Gemini powered assistant inside Google Maps, more helpful ahead of the holidays. It can now build step by step trip itineraries, give real time details about your route and find local appointments for you, whether it is a quick trip to the mall or a multi day road trip. The features are rolling out now, and Ask Maps is also expanding to thirty new countries across Europe. Google also published tips for beating holiday traffic based on Maps trends.

Next, CNBC reports that Nvidia's board has no plans to bring back Jensen Huang and is focused instead on the ongoing national security investigation. US officials are examining whether Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore's Megaspeed, helped divert Nvidia chips to China in violation of US export controls. The government has also moved to restrict remote access to AI chips, closing a loophole that let foreign buyers use US AI hardware through overseas data centers.

Then, Samsung is reportedly building a personal agent into its next Galaxy phones. Leaked code suggests the Galaxy S twenty seven lineup could include Galaxy Claw, an agent that handles tasks like making calls, sending texts and creating appointments. The phones are expected in early twenty twenty seven, but Samsung has not confirmed the feature.

From the frontier, Superhuman looks at how creatives can prove their work is real as AI content floods the internet. Graphite estimates AI now writes half of the internet's new articles, and people generate half a billion images a day with ChatGPT's image models. New tools aim to help: platforms like Workings, Wintertext and ObraVera verify writers by recording sessions, tracking typing patterns or fingerprinting the final work, while cameras like the CAIM one embed cryptographic proof that a human took each photo. Superhuman says such tools could matter most in places like college admissions, where verifying that students wrote their own essays has become a real challenge.
