November twenty eighth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Macy's Day Parade, Rocker the Coonhound, and Christmas Tree Guides.

The one hundredth Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade will be held this morning in New York City at eight thirty Eastern on NBC, after being rescheduled from Thursday because of severe weather. Organizers faced forecasts of peak winds near sixty miles an hour, a serious threat with one hundred large inflatables set to float through midtown Manhattan, and it marks the first postponement in the event's century long history. The parade, which began with Macy's employees, some costumes and a few live animals from the Central Park Zoo, now features as many as eleven thousand participants and more than two dozen floats, and in nineteen ninety seven a giant Cat in the Hat blew off course and injured four people. The Thanksgiving Day storm system blanketed the Midwest and East Coast, knocking out power to nearly one million customers, flooding and damaging buildings from Virginia to New Jersey, and canceling more than four hundred flights while delaying two thousand others. Meanwhile, residents along the California coast faced huge waves and significant flooding from a king tide.

The US military detonated aging lead explosives at an Iowa facility. Army officials said roughly ten thousand pounds of degraded lead azide, a volatile compound used in detonators, was at risk of exploding on its own, and measurements afterward showed minimal lead in the air.

Rocker the coonhound won the National Dog Show. The three year old black and tan became the first of his breed to take best in show in the event's history, beating runner up Kissy the Lhasa Apso, and he is expected to compete at the Westminster Dog Show at the end of January.

The Pittsburgh Steelers abruptly released star quarterback Aaron Rodgers. After being benched Tuesday amid a rift with coach Mike McCarthy, the Super Bowl winning quarterback gave a scorched earth interview on The Pat McAfee Show, accusing McCarthy of repeatedly falling asleep in team meetings, among other issues, and he was let go Wednesday afternoon.

Scientists developed nanorobots for drug delivery in the brain. The tiny devices, hundreds of times smaller than the width of a human hair, are injected into the bloodstream and triggered with infrared light to cross the blood brain barrier, and the approach could one day treat hard to reach conditions like glioblastoma.

The Trump administration proposed new tariffs on fourteen trading partners, including China, Japan and the European Union, aimed at countries with artificially high production capacity in certain manufacturing sectors.

And French actor Gérard Depardieu was charged with rape. The seventy seven year old, who has starred in more than two hundred films, is accused of raping actress Charlotte Arnould, then twenty two, in twenty eighteen, and he denies the charges after being separately convicted last year of sexually assaulting two women on set.
