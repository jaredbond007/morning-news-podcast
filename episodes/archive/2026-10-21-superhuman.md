From the frontier, financial media firm Citrini Research, a team of five with no scientists, tried to prompt Anthropic's Claude into creating a new medicine, a scorpion antivenom. In three months Claude produced two hundred eleven candidates, seventeen of which met Citrini's success criteria, using about three hundred sixty seven messages, ten thousand H one hundred GPU hours, an internal Anthropic model and a two hundred thousand dollar budget. Claude handled essentially everything, from initial ideas to emailing suppliers and writing its own prompts. The candidates stuck to their toxins but were never tested to see if they neutralize venom, and Citrini skipped animal studies, dosing and safety testing. Still, Citrini counted it a win, saying Claude did roughly a year of work in days.
