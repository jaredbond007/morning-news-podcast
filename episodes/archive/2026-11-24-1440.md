November twenty fourth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Huang Returns, Cartel Chaos, and the First Thanksgiving Menu.

Nvidia's board voted unanimously Sunday to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, days after ruling it out. President Trump took credit for the reversal, saying he assured the ten member board there was no evidence Huang had done anything illegal. Nvidia stock closed up thirty one and a half percent, the Nasdaq rose six and a half percent, and the S and P five hundred and Dow gained four point seven and three percent after ending last week in correction territory. The move follows reports of Huang's relationship with an alleged Chinese spy, who the New York Times reported Sunday was in contact with China's top spy agency during the affair, plus reported affairs with two Nvidia employees. Huang still faces an internal probe and several congressional investigations, and is due to testify before three House committees next week.

Mexico confirmed the US has requested the extradition of alleged cartel leader Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as El Chapo Isidro, one of the FBI's ten most wanted fugitives. His Friday arrest sparked weekend violence across the state of Sinaloa that left sixteen suspected cartel members and four soldiers dead. The forty four year old's drug trafficking history spans more than twenty years, and he most recently led Los Mazatlecos, a group accused of murder, torture, kidnapping and extortion. Mexican officials say a US surveillance drone helped in the arrest. Organized crime controls an estimated third of Mexico's territory, and analysts expect rivals to take over smuggling routes, limiting the arrest's effect on the drug trade.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy claimed credit for a drone attack on two Russian ships at a naval base in Libya, more than one thousand five hundred miles from the front lines. He said the operation used seven Ukrainian made naval drones launched from an undisclosed location. Kyiv has mostly targeted Russia's military and shadow fleet in the nearby Black and Azov seas, and the strike adds to signs Ukraine has built an intelligence network in the Mediterranean, following reports it operates in western Libya under a covert, Western backed deal. Libya has been split since twenty fourteen between a UN recognized government in the west and a military administration close to Moscow in the east. Separately, NATO will send an early warning aircraft to Poland after three Russian missiles landed there Friday.

And northern paper wasps have specialized neurons that help them recognize each other's faces, much like humans and other primates.
