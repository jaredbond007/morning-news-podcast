November fifth, twenty twenty six. The Rundown AI newsletter. AI agents can now make your phone calls.

First, SpaceXSI rolled out phone calls in Grok Bot, its AI agent platform, which can now sit on hold, book appointments and negotiate bills on US numbers. If a rep asks something the bot cannot answer, you get a message mid call and type a reply for it to pass along while following a live transcript. SpaceXSI's terms make users the caller of record and solely responsible for each call and for following phone laws, with bots disclosing they are a virtual assistant. Early examples include a Comcast bill negotiation and a pizza order. Grok Bot is for paid users, and Elon Musk answered complaints about usage limits by calling its cost unsustainably high relative to revenue. With Grok Voice already answering twenty thousand Starlink calls a day, The Rundown says phone lines could soon be bots talking to bots.
