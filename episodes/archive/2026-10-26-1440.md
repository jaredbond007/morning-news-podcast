October twenty sixth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Brazil Election, Starlink Returns, and the Science of Smiling.

First, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva won Brazil's presidential runoff yesterday, edging out conservative populist Flávio Bolsonaro after trailing him by two points in the first round earlier this month. The win secures the eighty year old leftist incumbent a fourth term. First elected in two thousand two, Lula left office at the end of twenty ten due to term limits, was jailed in twenty eighteen on corruption charges linked to Operation Car Wash, had his convictions nullified in twenty twenty one, and then beat Bolsonaro's father, Jair, in twenty twenty two. He now faces surging federal debt of about one point eight trillion dollars and frustration over public services and the rising cost of living. The race kicks off a string of major elections, including Israel's tomorrow and the US midterms next Tuesday.

Next, the Archdiocese of New York agreed to pay more than eight hundred million dollars to settle decades of sexual abuse claims against clergy and staff, avoiding a potential bankruptcy filing. The deal offers a minimum two hundred fifty thousand dollar payout to roughly one thousand three hundred plaintiffs, with some cases able to seek more. A flood of filings followed New York's twenty nineteen Child Victims Act, which opened a lookback window for expired claims and raised the age limit for civil suits from twenty three to fifty five. Similar settlements have been reached in Buffalo, Albany, Rochester, Syracuse and Rockville Centre, and since a pivotal two thousand two Boston Globe investigation, major settlements have topped five billion dollars and surfaced at least sixteen thousand abuse allegations.

And finally, Elon Musk accused Ukraine of violating Starlink rules by using it for offensive drones, his first acknowledgment of an active role in restricting Kyiv's satellite communications this month. Ukraine relies on both Starlink, a civilian service, and Starshield, a Pentagon funded program, but denies using Starlink offensively. Musk had earlier suggested the US government directed the slowdown, and a Reuters report said the decision came from the White House amid tensions over Ukraine's strikes on Russian oil infrastructure, which US officials denied. President Donald Trump reportedly called Musk last week asking SpaceX to restore full access, and Ukraine confirms both systems are now working normally, ahead of US, Russia and Ukraine talks due next month.

A study suggests mother bats respond to the high pitched isolation calls of their own individual pups, much like humans. And OpenAI resumed frontier model training but disconnected it from the live internet, citing risks from past agents' rogue behavior.
