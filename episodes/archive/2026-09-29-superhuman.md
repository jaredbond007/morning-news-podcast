Superhuman also looked at a different agent risk: what happens when agents work exactly as told. People are using agents to haggle down bills, cancel forgotten subscriptions and book reservations, and Business Insider reports the extra traffic is straining customer service lines built for human callers. Apollo's chief economist Torsten Slok warns of a bigger version: if every household told its agent to find the best checking account rate, deposits could flood out of lower paying banks all at once, effectively an agent driven bank run. The problem is scale, since a convenience for one person can overwhelm systems built for humans when millions do it at the same time.
