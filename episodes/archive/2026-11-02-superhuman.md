November second, twenty twenty six. The Superhuman newsletter. Whisper capturing glasses go viral.

First, startup Subvocal launched glasses that let you talk without making a sound. They send radar signals through your face while you mouth words, capturing tiny muscle movements and converting them into text at two hundred words per minute. That lets you dictate text, prompt AI, or even communicate with other Subvocal wearers without speaking out loud, and the demo racked up more than eleven million views.

Next, The Biological Computing Company, or TBC, revealed its first AI model, shaped by four years of research on how living brain cells compute.
