First, exit polls suggest no clear winner in Israel's first election since the Hamas led October seventh attacks and the war in Gaza, likely setting the stage for weeks of negotiations. Arab led parties emerged as the next largest bloc, and while both coalitions have refused to partner with them before, they may need to reconsider.
