October twenty sixth, twenty twenty six. The Rundown AI newsletter. China's Kimi continues to challenge the frontier.

First, Chinese AI lab Moonshot introduced Kimi K three point one Pro, an upgrade to its July K three model that it says matches the strongest proprietary models on key benchmarks, with free to download weights due by November fourth. Artificial Analysis ranks it fourth on its Intelligence Index, ahead of OpenAI's GPT six Astra and behind only Anthropic's five point five pair and Meta's Muse Wonder. In Moonshot's own tests it beats Opus five point five on hours long coding projects, expert academic questions, document heavy research and real world job tasks. It costs two dollars in and ten dollars out per million tokens, a fifth of Astra's rate, though it burns through tokens faster than its rivals. Artificial Analysis also ranks it the best openly available cyber defense model, and The Rundown notes Dario Amodei gave defenders six to twelve months in May before Chinese models catch up.

Then, Canva launched Craft, its first in house AI image model, debuting at number four on DesignArena's crowdsourced leaderboard behind only three OpenAI models. Craft was trained on millions of designer made templates like menus, slides and posters from Canva's own library. It plans each layout before drawing it, so images convert easily into editable designs, and its score beats the top models from Microsoft, Google and xAI while ranking first in portrait and nature. It is live for all Canva users and through a public API, putting near frontier image tools in front of more than two hundred fifty million users.
