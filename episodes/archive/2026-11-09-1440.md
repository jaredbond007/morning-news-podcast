November ninth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Russia Ukraine Talks, Krakow Shooting, and a TSA Spider.

Representatives from the US, Ukraine and Russia will meet in Abu Dhabi tomorrow for two days of talks, the fourth round of US brokered trilateral negotiations and the first since February. President Trump predicted an agreement could come soon, possibly involving all of Europe, after meeting a Russian envoy at the White House last week. Special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner are expected in the US delegation, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio suggested progress has already been made on what postwar security could look like. France said Europe will be represented, though it is unclear how involved it will be. Kyiv and Moscow kept trading drone and ballistic missile attacks over the weekend.

Polish prosecutors opened a terrorism investigation into a mass shooting in Krakow's main market square that killed at least six people and wounded fifteen, Poland's deadliest shooting since the post communist era began in nineteen eighty nine. Around three in the afternoon Saturday, a twenty four year old Polish man opened fire randomly with an automatic weapon from his car at Rynek Główny, a historic plaza that draws more than forty thousand visitors a day. Police later found the bodies of his father, mother and younger sister at his home about ninety three miles northeast of Krakow. The gunman, described as holding ultranationalist views, is in critical condition after being shot by police. Roughly one percent of Poland's thirty seven million people hold gun permits, compared with about thirty two percent of US adults who say they own a gun.

The Rockefeller Christmas tree arrived in New York City on Saturday, kicking off a season expected to draw up to seven hundred fifty thousand visitors a day. The tradition dates to nineteen thirty one, when construction workers pooled money for a twenty foot balsam fir. This year's tree is a seventy six foot, eleven ton Norway spruce from West Edmeston, New York, planted about seventy five years ago. It will be lit from December second to January ninth, topped by a roughly nine hundred pound Swarovski crystal star, and its wood will then go to Habitat for Humanity.

In science and technology. The US has passed five thousand measles cases, with infections in forty five states, and about ninety six percent of cases are among people who are unvaccinated or whose status is unknown. NASA opened astronaut applications for the first time since twenty twenty four, seeking recruits to help expand the Artemis program. And findings from a Siberian burial site suggest humans used cannabis for its psychoactive effects about two thousand eight hundred years ago, at least three hundred years earlier than previously documented.

In business and markets. US stocks closed mixed, with the S and P five hundred up three tenths of a percent, the Dow down half a percent and the Nasdaq up one point one percent as chip stocks rebounded from the post election selloff. Nvidia's board says it will investigate CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with an alleged Chinese spy after Huang told staff the two had a romantic relationship. And Poppi's founders, who sold their soda company to PepsiCo for nearly two billion dollars in twenty twenty five, launched Jetty, a hydration drink and electrolyte mix brand taking on Gatorade.

In politics and world affairs. UFC CEO Dana White will meet President Trump at the White House today, after Trump said he was kidding about offering White the press secretary job. Israel's president gave opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot the first chance to form a government after the October twenty seventh elections. Hundreds of protesters and counterprotesters turned out in Philadelphia amid a boycott of several Jewish owned businesses described as pro Israel. And Iran executed three men who took part in anti government protests this year, accusing them of violent unrest and supporting the US and Israel.
