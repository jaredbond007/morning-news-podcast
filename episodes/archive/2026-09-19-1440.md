September nineteenth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Warren Buffett, UFO Files, and Haunted Hotels.

Warren Buffett stepped down as chairman of Berkshire Hathaway yesterday, with his eldest son Howard Buffett succeeding him. At ninety six, Buffett stays on as chairman emeritus and as a board member of the conglomerate he led for more than six decades. He started buying shares in nineteen sixty two, when Berkshire was a struggling textile manufacturer, became chairman and chief executive in nineteen seventy, and turned it into an investment holding company. In twenty twenty four it became the first American company outside of technology to be valued above one trillion dollars. His partner Greg Abel took over as chief executive back in January, though Buffett reportedly kept coming into the office every day, and it is not clear yet whether that changes. Shares closed up one tenth of a percent on the news. Buffett has given away roughly sixty six billion dollars of stock since two thousand six, and his three children are responsible for distributing the rest of his roughly one hundred fifty billion dollar fortune by the end of twenty thirty four.
