November fourteenth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Brain Atlas, Cruise Ship Collision, and the Safest Cities.

A global consortium of more than fifty institutions has built the most detailed cellular atlas yet of the basal ganglia, the brain region most tied to disease, which could inspire treatments for a range of neurological and psychiatric conditions. Researchers profiled twenty five and a half million cells from human, macaque and marmoset brains, with findings spread across twenty six papers published this week and the data made public. The basal ganglia are interconnected structures deep in the brain that shape movement, habits, decision making and emotion, and damage there has been linked to Parkinson's disease, schizophrenia and bipolar disorder. The atlas has already turned up a previously unknown brain cell type tied to schizophrenia and bipolar disorder, plus genetic changes in specific cells that may contribute to autism. Scientists call it a major step toward mapping the entire human brain.

The Federal Aviation Administration grounded flights nationwide for about four and a half hours yesterday, citing safety concerns later tied to an outdated air traffic control system. It was only the second nationwide ground stop since September eleventh, two thousand one, after one in twenty twenty three.

In New Orleans, an oil tanker struck a docked river cruise ship carrying about one hundred eighty people as it finished an eight day Mississippi River cruise, capsizing it. Everyone is accounted for, and thirty two people were hospitalized, none critically. The cause is still unclear.

Maryland Democratic Senator Chris Van Hollen says he will challenge Chuck Schumer, who has led Senate Democrats for nearly ten years, in a secret ballot election set for early December. Democrats' last contested race for the post, in nineteen ninety four, was decided by one vote. Separately, Republican Representative Lauren Boebert lost her reelection bid to retired Navy Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher, widening the Democrats' House majority.

Microsoft blamed a technical issue, not AI or a cyberattack, for a global outage of its workplace software that began Thursday. It hit police reporting systems, health services, courts and schools across seventy four countries and territories.

Researchers developed a tongue swab tuberculosis test that gives results in twenty minutes, compared with up to three days for skin and blood tests. It needs little training and withstands heat up to one hundred thirteen degrees, making it suited to remote areas where the disease is most common.

More than half of this year's living Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inductees are expected to miss tonight's ceremony in Los Angeles, citing scheduling conflicts, health issues and band disputes. Billy Idol and the Wu Tang Clan are expected to perform, and an edited version airs on ABC and Disney Plus in December.

And Washington Commanders practice squad quarterback Sam Hartman, twenty seven, will lead the thirtieth season of The Bachelor, the first active pro athlete to star on the show. It premieres March twenty first, ending a yearlong hiatus that followed the canceled Bachelorette season starring Taylor Frankie Paul.
