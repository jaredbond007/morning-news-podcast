October seventeenth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Flying Car, Oregon School Shooting, and Grown up Happy Meals.

First, Tesla unveiled its new Roadster this week, touting it as one of the world's first flying cars. The electric sports car has thrusters developed with SpaceX that let it briefly go airborne, and it can reportedly go from zero to sixty in under one second and top two hundred thirty miles per hour. Thursday's demonstration showed it flipping off a curved ramp and leaping over five other Teslas. Production is slated for twenty twenty eight at a price above two hundred thousand dollars, though the flying version is not approved for public roads and regulators are unsure which agency would oversee it. The fascination dates back to at least the eighteen fifties, when French sea captain Jean Marie Le Bris designed an albatross inspired glider.

In quick hits. A bystander shot and killed the gunman in the Oregon school shooting. Kaylub Giamanco, a thirty six year old carpenter with a concealed weapon, intervened at the school his two daughters attend, after the sixteen year old suspect shot through locked doors and killed a teacher and three students in a special education classroom.

President Trump reportedly fired press secretary Katie Zacharia a week after appointing her, after questions arose about her status as a lawyer. She has a law degree but failed the bar exam.

Sean Diddy Combs is being moved to a federal prison in Pennsylvania after a reported fight and reports of smuggled Hennessy, a contraband phone and inmates cooking and cleaning for him. He lost forty one days of good conduct credit, pushing his release to March second, twenty twenty eight.

The CDC is sending a disease detective to help contain Pennsylvania's measles outbreak. The state averaged over one hundred new cases a week last month and accounts for more than a quarter of the nation's more than four thousand four hundred cases.

Israeli settlers attacked a West Bank village's olive harvest, burning trees and throwing rocks and wounding at least two people, including a Washington Post photojournalist. Settlers have launched at least one hundred seventy attacks since the harvest began September twenty seventh.

The WNBA Finals tip off today, with the Golden State Valkyries hosting the Atlanta Dream, amid record viewership and attendance. Games three and four were moved to Atlanta's seventeen thousand seat State Farm Arena.

The Milwaukee Brewers reached the World Series for the first time in forty four years after beating the two time defending champion Los Angeles Dodgers. They host the opener on October twenty third.

A landmark study found ovaries remain active decades after menopause, with tissue changes and inflammation, challenging the assumption that they go dormant.

And the nation's leading OB GYN group issued its first updated menopause guidance in over a decade, strongly recommending hormone replacement therapy for hot flashes and night sweats.
