October tenth, twenty twenty six. The 1440 Daily Digest newsletter. Nobel Peace Prize, Wine Heist, and a Rumpelstiltskin Meme.

First, the Nobel Committee awarded its Peace Prize to former UN human rights chief Navi Pillay. The eighty five year old South African born lawyer began her career fighting apartheid and became the first nonwhite woman on South Africa's High Court after being nominated by Nelson Mandela, whom she had defended. On the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda, she helped establish sexual violence as a form of genocide. The committee chair also cited her recent work leading the UN commission on Gaza, which concluded Israel committed genocide against Palestinians. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu condemned the decision, President Donald Trump reshared a post claiming he deserved the prize, and hours later his administration sanctioned the International Criminal Court, where Pillay once served as a judge.

In other news. Former Chicago Bears coach and Hall of Fame tight end Mike Ditka has died at eighty six. He is the only person to have contributed to the Bears' last two league championships, as a player in nineteen sixty three and head coach in nineteen eighty five, and was the first tight end inducted into the Hall of Fame.

Panama was struck by a seven point seven magnitude earthquake, collapsing dozens of buildings and prompting nationwide evacuations, followed by a six point six aftershock. Several injuries but no deaths have been reported.

The Trump administration struck a deal to get diesel from Russia to ease fuel prices, with diesel averaging six dollars and twenty eight cents a gallon. It comes less than a month after Trump signed a sweeping sanctions bill meant to pressure Moscow over Ukraine.

Trump also set up a committee to investigate mortgage fraud allegations against Federal Reserve governor Lisa Cook and whether they justify firing her, with a November fifth hearing. The Supreme Court blocked an earlier attempt to fire her. Separately, conservative commentator Katie Zacharia will replace Karoline Leavitt as White House press secretary.

The Pentagon will livestream the execution of Nidal Hasan, the former Army psychiatrist who killed thirteen people at Fort Hood in two thousand nine. Set for December third, it would be the first US military firing squad execution since nineteen forty five, and the first publicly broadcast.

Iran backed Houthi rebels struck Riyadh's airport, killing three Saudis including an airline pilot, the deadliest attack since Saudi Arabia launched an offensive with Yemen's government last week.

And thieves stole thirty thousand bottles, about five point eight million dollars' worth, from Italian winemaker Marchesi Antinori, in the country's largest wine heist. They allegedly disabled surveillance and used the winery's own forklifts. Separately, French authorities recovered two stolen Renoir paintings and arrested six suspects.
